Close All Now for Mac

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

The short version

Close All Now does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the app does with information

  • The app makes no network connections. Nothing leaves your Mac.
  • There are no analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app.
  • The app reads the list of applications that are currently open so that it can show them and ask them to quit. That list is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Your list of excluded applications and your start-on-login choice are stored on your Mac in the app's own preferences. Deleting the app's data removes them.
  • The helper script you install lives in ~/Library/Application Scripts/com.schwedhelm.closeallnow/. It only passes on quit requests to the apps you chose to close.

Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
